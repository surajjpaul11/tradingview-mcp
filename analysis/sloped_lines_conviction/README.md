# Sloped Lines entry-conviction study

## Scope

This study evaluates every completed long trade generated by the configured Sloped Lines parameters for AAPL, AMD, NVDA, and SPY on five years of daily Yahoo Finance candles (2021-09-24 through 2026-09-23). End-of-data marks are excluded. Returns include the backtest's 0.30% round-trip cost assumption.

The resulting dataset contains 575 closed trades. `entry_features.csv` provides one row per buy/sell pair, including entry and exit dates, net return, RSI(14), 20-day relative volume, trend context, candle shape, and available trendline geometry. `summary.json` contains reproducible grouped results.

## Main result

Unusually high volume on the signal candle is the clearest candidate, especially when the breakout candle closes green and in the upper 30% of its range. RSI is useful as supporting context but is not a reliable standalone filter.

| Entry condition | Trades | Win rate | Mean net return | Median net return |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| All entries | 575 | 37.9% | +1.52% | -0.30% |
| Signal-day volume at least 1.2x prior 20-day average | 120 | 50.0% | +3.63% | -0.02% |
| Signal-day volume at least 1.2x and RSI at least 50 | 74 | 52.7% | +3.48% | +1.32% |
| Signal-day volume at least 1.2x, green candle, close in top 30% | 81 | 63.0% | +6.02% | +3.14% |
| Red candle or close in bottom 30% | 104 | 18.3% | -1.27% | -1.33% |

The strong-candle condition also held in the latest chronological 30% of trades: 19 wins from 31 entries (61.3%), with a +9.00% mean. That mean is heavily influenced by a few large AMD winners, so win rate and median return are more informative than the mean.

On the current one-year dashboard window, the four configurations produced 98 completed trades with a 43.9% pooled win rate. Signal-day volume of at least 1.2x occurred 22 times and won 14 times (63.6%), so the recent window points in the same direction, although it is too small and was involved in parameter selection.

## RSI crossing above 50

An RSI-50 crossover was defined before looking at the results: the prior completed daily RSI(14) is below 50, the signal-day RSI closes at or above 50, and the signal-day close is higher than the prior close. There were 67 such entries.

The crossover raised the percentage of profitable strategy exits but did not predict a larger subsequent price increase:

| Measurement from next open | All entries | RSI cross above 50 | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Profitable strategy exits | 33.4% | 43.3% | +9.9 pp |
| Mean strategy return | +0.41% | -0.14% | -0.55 pp |
| Positive after 1 trading day | 53.0% | 52.2% | -0.8 pp |
| Mean 1-day price return | +0.15% | -0.35% | -0.50 pp |
| Positive after 5 trading days | 57.0% | 49.3% | -7.7 pp |
| Mean 5-day price return | +0.65% | -0.54% | -1.19 pp |
| Positive after 10 trading days | 57.1% | 47.0% | -10.1 pp |
| Mean 10-day price return | +1.53% | +0.41% | -1.12 pp |
| Positive after 20 trading days | 56.6% | 42.2% | -14.4 pp |
| Mean 20-day price return | +2.47% | -0.33% | -2.80 pp |

Compared directly with entries that did not have the crossover, the estimated mean-return difference was -0.57 percentage points after one day, -1.34 after five days, -1.27 after ten days, and -3.16 after twenty days. Bootstrap intervals were wide at the shorter horizons, but the 20-day 95% interval barely excluded zero (-6.21 to -0.02 percentage points). This is evidence against using the crossover as a general higher-return signal in the present dataset.

The result differs by stock. AAPL had favorable 1- and 20-day behavior, AMD was favorable around 10 days but reversed by 20 days, NVDA crossover entries had negative mean returns at every tested horizon, and SPY showed no useful improvement. Allowing the crossover to have occurred during any of the prior three sessions did not repair the pooled result.

In the current one-year window, crossover entries won 8 of 16 strategy trades (50.0%) from the next open versus 36.7% for all entries, but their mean strategy return was +1.37% versus +2.45% overall. The sample supports a possible increase in win frequency, not an increase in expected return. The two-proportion comparison is not statistically conclusive (five-year two-sided p = 0.068; one-year p = 0.229).

The practical conclusion is to display an RSI-50 crossover as context rather than award it a positive conviction weight. It may become useful in a stock-specific model, particularly for AAPL, but NVDA should not receive the same treatment. Combining the crossover with high volume did not improve the next-open results.

## Timing caveat

The current strategy can register a daily breakout from the completed candle but record its fill at the projected line or that day's open. Final volume, closing location, and RSI are only known at the close, so using those values with the current fill price creates a timing mismatch.

A conservative approximation that waits until the next day's open reduced the strong-candle condition to 47 wins from 81 entries (58.0%) and a +2.98% mean. Results varied materially by stock:

| Stock | All-entry win rate | Strong-candle trades | Strong-candle win rate at next open | Mean next-open return |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| AAPL | 33.9% | 21 | 71.4% | +1.16% |
| AMD | 35.5% | 20 | 70.0% | +11.27% |
| NVDA | 23.6% | 24 | 37.5% | -0.36% |
| SPY | 47.6% | 16 | 56.2% | +0.00% |

This is promising for AAPL and AMD, but it is not a universal conviction rule. AMD's large winners dominate pooled average returns, while NVDA and SPY do not show a positive next-open expectancy under this condition.

## What was known before the signal candle

Filters based only on the prior completed daily candle were much weaker:

| Prior-bar condition | Trades | Win rate | Mean net return | Median net return |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| RSI 50 to 60 | 149 | 42.3% | +1.86% | -0.30% |
| Volume at least 1.2x prior 20-day average | 106 | 40.6% | +3.05% | -0.30% |
| RSI at least 50 and volume at least 1.2x | 44 | 43.2% | +2.18% | -0.30% |

The prior-bar RSI/volume combination did not remain stable in the later sample. In particular, it produced zero wins from two AAPL trades and zero wins from four NVDA trades in each stock's chronological holdout. There is not enough evidence to label it a high-conviction filter.

## Other parameters reviewed

Trendline drop, anchor spacing, days since the second anchor, breakout distance, prior 5- and 20-day momentum, ATR%, and the 200-day moving-average regime were examined. None showed a consistent pooled relationship strong enough to use as a conviction score. A pooled preference for shallower trendlines was an artifact of the different stock-specific configurations, especially SPY, and did not generalize within each stock.

The next useful inputs to test are:

1. **Market-relative strength:** stock return minus SPY and sector-ETF return over 5, 20, and 60 days.
2. **Intraday volume pace:** volume accumulated by the breakout time divided by expected volume for that time of day. This avoids waiting for final daily volume.
3. **Gap and execution quality:** next-open gap from the breakout close, spread, and dollar volume.
4. **Trend quality:** ADX, 50/200-day slope, and distance from the 20/50-day averages.
5. **Market regime:** SPY trend, volatility regime (VIX or realized volatility), and broad-market breadth.
6. **Event risk:** earnings proximity and large overnight news gaps.
7. **Trade excursion:** maximum favorable and adverse excursion after entry, which can separate entry quality from the exit rule.

## Recommended implementation

Do not label the current result as a probability of profit yet. Add an experimental conviction badge with three transparent components: relative volume, breakout-candle quality, and RSI context. Calculate it only after a completed candle and model execution at the next tradable price. Keep the stock-specific evidence, sample size, and uncertainty visible.

Before enabling it as a trade filter, rerun the whole stateful strategy with the filter applied, use walk-forward parameter selection, reserve a final untouched test period, and compare opportunity cost from skipped entries. The current results are exploratory because the Sloped Lines parameters were already selected using part of this history.
