Curved Channel Strategy — Chart Legend Reference

Complete guide to every marker abbreviation, entry/exit mechanism, and version tag shown on backtest charts.

Entry Marker Abbreviations

These appear on chart markers as BUY <ABBREV> or SHORT <ABBREV>. Each tells you exactly what mechanism triggered the position.

Requires volume confirmation, 2-bar channel presence, channel maturity, and SMA trend alignment. Exit Marker Abbreviations

These appear on chart markers as EXIT <ABBREV> or COVER <ABBREV>. Each tells you why the position was closed.

Marker Text Abbreviation Full Name Description & How It Helps Versions
EXIT ATR-TS ATR-TS ATR Trailing Stop Price hit the ATR-based trailing stop. For longs, the stop trails 2.0x ATR below the highest high since entry. For shorts, 1.5x ATR above the lowest low. The stop only moves in the favorable direction. Locks in profits while giving the trade room to breathe. Tighter for shorts (1.5x) since downtrends tend to be sharper. v2 (longs)   v2v23 (shorts)
EXIT CH-TS CH-TS Channel Trail Stop The in-channel trailing stop triggered. Activates after the position reaches +2% profit while still inside a channel. Uses a tighter 1.5x ATR trail to protect gains without waiting for a full channel break. Captures quick channel profits instead of giving them back — especially effective in choppy ascending channels. v9
EXIT BRK-UP BRK-UP Breakout Up Price broke above the upper channel boundary by more than the breakout threshold. The existing long is closed so it can be immediately re-entered at a larger size (200%) with breakout momentum trailing. Not a loss exit — this closes a profitable channel position to re-open bigger for the breakout. v2
EXIT BRK-DN BRK-DN Breakout Down Price broke below the lower channel boundary by more than the breakout threshold. For longs this is a protective exit; a short may immediately follow if conditions are met. Cuts losses quickly on channel breakdowns rather than hoping for a bounce. v2
EXIT CH-BRK CH-BRK Channel Break Price closed below channel support (for longs) or above channel resistance (for shorts) by more than the touch tolerance, but less than the breakout threshold. A moderate violation that invalidates the channel thesis. Exits on the smaller channel violations that don't qualify as full breakouts. —
EXIT CH-FLIP CH-FLIP Channel Flip The active channel type changed direction while a position was open. For example, holding a long when the channel flips from ascending to descending. Prevents holding a position against the new trend direction when market structure reverses. —
EXIT CH-EXP CH-EXP Channel Expired The polynomial channel expired (no more projected data points) and the position was not profitable enough to ride with an ATR trail. Profitable positions are kept open with trailing stop management. Only exits losing positions on channel expiry — winners ride on with ATR protection (v10). v10
EXIT TIME TIME Time Exit Position was held for the maximum allowed bars (default 20) without reaching +1% profit. Prevents capital from being tied up in stagnant trades. Frees capital from dead trades so it can be deployed in new opportunities. —
EXIT SMA-X SMA-X Fallback SMA Exit A position entered via the fallback SMA mechanism was closed because price dropped back below SMA(50). Requires a minimum 3-bar hold before the exit can trigger. Exits SMA-based trades when the simple trend signal reverses, with a hold buffer to avoid whipsaws. v5v32
EXIT EOD EOD End of Data The backtest reached the last available candle and the open position was force-closed at the final close price. Not a strategy signal. Ensures all positions are accounted for in the backtest results. Ignore for strategy evaluation. —

Strategy Version Tags

Version tags appear in the Trade Log panel's second row (e.g. v8 v25 v48). Each tag references an improvement that was tested, kept, and contributes to that specific trade decision.

Tag Label Type Description & How It Helps
v1 Full Channel Entry Filter Widened entry zone to the full channel width (support to resistance) instead of requiring price near the boundary. Catches more entries that would have been missed by a narrow boundary-only trigger.
v2 Breakout Detect Exit Type Detects when price breaks beyond the channel boundary by a threshold percentage and activates an ATR trailing stop. Also enables breakout re-entry at larger position size. The foundation for all breakout-related entries and exits. Turns channel violations into trading opportunities.
v3 Short SMA Filter Entry Filter Only allows short entries when price is below SMA(200). Prevents shorting in strong uptrends. Dramatically reduces losing short trades by ensuring you only short when the long-term trend is bearish.
v5 Fallback SMA Entry Filter When no channel is detected, uses SMA(50) crossover as a simpler entry signal after waiting for a cooldown period. Eliminates long flat periods with zero exposure. Keeps capital working even when channels are absent.
v8 Volume Confirm Entry Filter Requires volume to be at least 1.2x its 20-bar moving average before allowing entry. Filters out low-conviction entries. Higher volume at entry confirms institutional participation.
v9 Channel Trail Exit Type Activates a tighter trailing stop (1.5x ATR) once a channel position reaches +2% profit. Locks in channel profits before they can evaporate. Especially effective in choppy channels that oscillate within the boundaries.
v10 Ride Winners Exit Type When a channel expires, profitable positions are kept open with ATR trailing stop management instead of being force-closed. Lets winning trades run beyond channel expiry instead of cutting them at an arbitrary point.
v21 Channel Age Entry Filter Requires channels to exist for at least 3 bars before allowing entry. Applied to ETFs only (crypto channels are more volatile and form faster). Prevents entries on freshly-detected channels that may be false positives from noisy data.
v23 Tight Short Trail Exit Type Uses a tighter 1.5x ATR trailing stop for short positions (vs 2.0x for longs). Shorts tend to reverse faster than longs, so a tighter trail captures more downside profit before the bounce.
v25 2-Bar Confirm Entry Filter Requires the previous bar's close to also be inside the channel before allowing entry. Ensures price is genuinely within the channel rather than just touching it on a single spike.
v30 Crypto Breakout Channel Detection Lowers the breakout threshold from 2.0% to 1.5% for crypto assets. Crypto channels tend to be wider, so a lower percentage still represents significant momentum.
v32 SMA Min Hold Exit Type Requires a minimum 3-bar hold before a fallback SMA exit can trigger. Prevents whipsaw exits where price briefly dips below SMA(50) and immediately recovers.
v39 Crypto Extend Channel Detection Extends projected channel lifetime to 30 bars for crypto (vs 20 for ETFs). Crypto trends run longer, so channels need more room to project forward without expiring prematurely.
v48 SMA Trend Filter Entry Filter Requires close to be above SMA(50) * 0.99 for ascending channel long entries. The 0.99 buffer allows entries slightly below the SMA. Aligns channel entries with the medium-term trend. The 1% buffer was the sweet spot — strict (1.00) hurt QQQ, softer (0.98) had no effect.
v49 Poly-Low Entry Entry Filter Fits a degree-2 polynomial through the last 12 bar lows. Enters a 25% long when the slope at the right edge is positive and volume ≥80% of its MA. Channel-gated: only active before the first channel forms. One-shot per backtest. Closes the gap between B&H start and first strategy trade by detecting upward-curving lows before polynomial channels can form.
v51 Steep Reversal Entry Filter Counter-trend entry when recent 5-bar slope exceeds 20-bar baseline by Nx. Confirmation via lower-low (short) or higher-high (long). Disabled by default — testing showed counter-trend 25% bets on daily bars don't generate positive alpha. Code retained for potential intraday use where mean reversion is more reliable.
v50 Sharp Reversal Entry Filter Enters a 25% position when a single bar moves ≥2x ATR with ≥2x average volume, following bars in the opposite direction. Can fire multiple times whenever the position is flat. Captures high-conviction reversal events that channel detection is too slow to react to — V-bottoms and distribution spikes.
