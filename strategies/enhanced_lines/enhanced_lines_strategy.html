Scene 3: Trend Flip — SELL 100%
Trendline slopes disagree or both turn down → trend is no longer up.
This is a regime change, not a swing. We SELL 100% of all holdings immediately, regardless of volume.
After the full exit, we wait for a downtrend channel to form before entering shorts.

Scene 6: Horizontal / Neutral — Lines Disagree or Both Flat
When support and resistance slopes disagree (one up, one down = converging/diverging wedge) or both are nearly flat → trend is NEUTRAL.
If we were long → SELL 100% (same as trend flip — regime change).
If we were short → CLOSE ALL SHORTS.
Stay flat (no positions) until a clear trend re-establishes. The chart shows a converging wedge where support slopes up but resistance slopes down — lines disagree → neutral.

Volume-Weighted Position Sizing

Uses the 2 confirmation candles from the bounce detection. Higher volume = more conviction = larger trade.

avg_vol = mean(vol[confirm_bar_1], vol[confirm_bar_2])
vol_ratio = avg_vol / SMA(volume, 20)
trade_pct = clamp(vol_ratio × 25%, floor=20%, ceiling=80%)

Exception: Trend flip → always 100% (regime change, not a swing trade)
