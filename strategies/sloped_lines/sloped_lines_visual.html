Sloped Lines Strategy

Alternating trendline breakout — descending resistance (green) and ascending support (blue)

Full Cycle: Resistance Break → Buy → Support Break → Sell
The strategy alternates between two phases. Phase 1 (Green line): price is falling — draw a descending resistance line through two lower swing highs. When price breaks above → BUY. Phase 2 (Blue line): holding — draw an ascending support line through two higher swing lows. When price breaks below → SELL. Then repeat.

Phase 1: Descending Resistance → BUY
While price is falling, identify two consecutive swing highs where the second is lower than the first (lower highs). Draw a downward-sloping line through them. Validate that no candle close breaks above the line between the two anchors. Extend the line forward. When a candle closes above the projected line → trigger a BUY.

How the Strategy Works — Step by Step

  1. Start out of market — waiting for a buy signal
  2. Detect swing highs using pivot logic (5 bars left/right)
  3. Find two swing highs where the second is lower → descending resistance line
  4. Validate: no candle close breaks above the line between the two anchors
  5. Extend the line forward into future bars
  6. When price closes above the projected resistance → BUY
  7. Now holding — switch to watching for sell signals
  8. Detect swing lows that form after the buy
  9. Find two swing lows where the second is higher → ascending support line
  10. Validate: no candle close breaks below the line between the two anchors
  11. When price closes below the projected support → SELL
  12. Go back to step 2 — repeat the cycle with new swing points after the sell

Key Differences from Straight Line Strategy

Straight Line requires 4+ touches (2 anchors + 2 confirmations) to validate a trendline, then trades the break. Sloped Lines uses only 2 anchor points but validates that nothing breaks through between them. It's simpler and more responsive — fewer touches needed, faster signals, but the validation (no close violates the line) ensures quality. Both strategies trade trendline breaks, but Sloped Lines draws the line with just two points while Straight Line needs four.
