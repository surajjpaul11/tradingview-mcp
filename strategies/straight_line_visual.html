Straight Line Strategy

Trendline break detection with 4-point confirmation

Uptrend: Support Trendline Break = SELL
A support trendline is drawn through ascending swing lows. The line is anchored at the first two prominent lows and extended forward. When 2+ additional swing lows confirm the trendline (land within 1.5% tolerance), it becomes active. A candle closing below the trendline triggers a sell signal.

How the Strategy Works — Step by Step

  1. Detect swing lows and swing highs using pivot logic (5 bars left/right)
  2. In an uptrend: pick 2 ascending swing lows as anchor points and draw a straight line through them
  3. Extend the line forward — check if 2+ more swing lows land within 1.5% of the projected line
  4. Once confirmed (4+ total touches), the trendline is active
  5. When a candle closes below the trendline → SELL on the next bar's open
  6. After selling, look for a downtrend resistance trendline to form (descending swing highs)
  7. When price closes above the resistance trendline → BUY on the next bar's open
  8. Repeat — the strategy alternates between support and resistance trendline tracking

Tolerance Zone

Swing points rarely land exactly on a mathematical line. The 1.5% tolerance means a swing low at $98.50 would still "touch" a trendline projecting $100.00 at that bar (within 1.5%). This captures real-world trendlines where price bounces near — but not exactly on — support/resistance.
