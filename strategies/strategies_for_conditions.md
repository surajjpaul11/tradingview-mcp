## 2. Current Macro Theme: Oil Supply Disruption & Energy Transition

### What's Happening

- **Iran/Strait of Hormuz tensions** → oil supply risk → crude prices elevated
- **Oil tanker rerouting** → longer shipping routes (around Africa vs through Hormuz) → tanker day rates spiking
- **US energy independence push** → domestic producers benefit from higher prices
- **EV adoption accelerating** → structural shift away from oil long-term
- **Nuclear renaissance** → SMRs getting regulatory approval, uranium demand surging

### The Dual Play: Short-term Oil Bull + Long-term Energy Transition

This creates a rare opportunity to play **both sides**:

**SHORT-TERM (6-18 months): Oil supply squeeze**
- Oil producers benefit from high prices
- Tankers benefit from rerouting
- Defense contractors benefit from escalation

**LONG-TERM (2-5 years): Energy transition**
- EV manufacturers gain market share
- Nuclear/SMR becomes baseload power
- Uranium miners supply constrained
- Cybersecurity critical for energy infrastructure

## 6. Newly Added Strategies — Quick Reference

### Smart Hold RC (Reversal Channel variant)
- **Upgrade over Smart Hold** for volatile stocks (ATR% > 2.5%)
- Same proven exit logic (MA breakdown, VIX acceleration, 6x ATR trailing stop)
- High-vol re-entry: waits for Reversal Channel structural HH/HL confirmation (avoids dead cat bounces)
- Low-vol re-entry: standard Smart Hold MA reclaim (captures V-shaped recoveries faster)
- **SPY:** +44.95% vs +36.01% B&H, 7 trades, 71.4% win rate, Sharpe 3.75
- **Best for:** CRWD, PANW, SMR, BE, OKLO — anything with ATR% > 2.5%

### Curved Channel
- Polynomial (degree 2) channel fitting on pivot highs/lows
- Buys support bounces, sells resistance bounces, with ATR trailing stops
- 52 improvement iterations, tested across 26 symbols
- Short support via SMA(200) filter (shorts only when price < SMA 200)
- **Best for:** Stocks in well-defined channels — SPY, QQQ, established large-caps

### RSI Volume Watch
- High-conviction entry filter: RSI(14) crosses 50 + volume >= 1.3x its 20-bar MA
- Very selective — only 2 trades on SPY in 2 years (8.4% time in market)
- ATR trailing stop (3x) + 40-bar max hold + RSI fade exit
- Profit factor 1.70, max drawdown -3.65%
- **Best for:** Complementary signal — use alongside other strategies for confirmation, not as primary

### VWMA 17
- VWMA(17) price crossover with adaptive Kaufman Efficiency Ratio filter
- ER > 0.3 (trending) → requires price > SMA(200); ER <= 0.3 (choppy) → SMA(100) filter
- Full long + short support with ATR-based SL (1.5x) and TP (2.0x)
- **Best for:** Assets that alternate between trending and ranging — indices, large-cap tech

### Regime-Based Master Strategy
- Meta-framework that detects market regime and activates the appropriate sub-strategy:
  - **Trend** (ADX > 25): Hull Suite moving average
  - **Mean reversion** (ADX < 25): Inertial RSI fade extremes
  - **Volatility squeeze**: Donchian breakout after Bollinger compression
  - **Macro rotation**: Relative Strength sector switching
- ATR Volatility Ratio acts as risk governor (halves position size if vol > 1.5x)
- **Best for:** Portfolios or when you can't determine the regime yourself
- **Note:** Currently Pine Script indicators only — no unified Python backtester yet
